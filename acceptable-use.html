Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

You will not use Sky Waker, Composer, PCGen, our Discord, or our APIs to:

  1. Break the law, including child sexual abuse material (we will report), trafficking, malware, or credible threats.
  2. Steal the product — scrape or bulk-download library clips, rip shaders, share Composer logins, stand up a competing host from our files, or bypass login / XR lease / upload gates.
  3. Attack the service or other patrons’ folders.
  4. Upload media you do not have rights to, or use another living person’s likeness to harass them.
  5. Spam, phish, or run scams in our spaces.
  6. Target people with hate or harassment. Ordinary dark or noisy art is not automatically a ban.
  7. Automate PCGen to burn GPU quota or crash the worker.
  8. Impersonate DG MUN, staff, or another patron.
  9. Use Composer if you are under 18.
  10. Resell access or run a venue house system without a written license.

We may warn, suspend, delete projects, revoke leases, or ban. Report abuse via dgmun.ai, the copyright address, or a Discord moderator.
