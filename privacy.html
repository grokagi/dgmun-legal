Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This policy describes how DG MUN (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with dgmun.com, Patreon creator offerings (including tier-gated web tools), Discord community spaces we operate, and related developer applications registered with third-party platforms (e.g. Patreon developer / OAuth clients).

1. What we collect

  • Patreon. If you support or follow our work on Patreon, Patreon processes payments and account data under Patreon’s Privacy Policy. We receive what Patreon exposes to creators (for example pledge status and identifiers needed to provide tier benefits).
  • Tier-gated web access. If we offer hosted software (e.g. Sky Waker) verified by Patreon membership, we may process Patreon-related identifiers or tokens needed to confirm your tier and maintain a session. We do not use this to sell your data.
  • Discord. If you join our Discord server, Discord processes account data under Discord’s Privacy Policy. We may see your Discord user ID, messages you post in our server, and roles assigned via Patreon integration or moderators.
  • Contact. If you email us or contact us through a form, we keep that correspondence as long as needed to respond and operate our projects.
  • Site technical data. Hosting providers may log IP addresses, user agent, and similar data for security and operations. We keep analytics minimal; update this section if you add analytics cookies.

2. How we use information

To provide tier benefits, operate communities, improve our software, comply with law, and secure our services.

3. Sharing

We use service providers as needed (e.g. Patreon, Discord, hosting). We do not sell personal information.

4. Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

5. Your choices

You may disconnect third-party apps (e.g. Patreon↔Discord) in those services’ settings. For requests about data we hold, contact us (see below).

6. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13.
