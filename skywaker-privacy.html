Sky Waker privacy notice

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Sky Waker XR™ is run by Avidigi Agency LLC. This notice says in plain words what the Sky Waker app keeps about you, who else handles it, how long it stays, and how to download or delete it. Our general Privacy Policy covers the rest. Where the two differ, this notice applies to Sky Waker.

What we keep, and why

Your account

When you sign in with Patreon, Patreon tells us your name, your email, your Patreon user number and your membership, so we know you are a member. We never see your Patreon password or your card.

Your age group

The first time you sign in, we ask for your birth month and year (and the day, if you turn 13 or 18 that month). We keep only your age group (13 to 17, or 18 and over) and when you answered, never the date itself. If you are 13 to 17, you also confirm that a parent or guardian has said yes, and we keep when you did.

Your work

Your compositions, settings, characters and clips are kept in your own private folder, for you. Other members can't see them unless you share them.

Clips made from your videos

When you make a clip, your video comes to our server, which turns it into still frames. The frames go to RunPod, whose GPUs run the depth model that turns them into a 3D point cloud. The model estimates how far away each part of the picture is. It doesn't recognise faces or identify anyone. Large sets of frames pass through a private storage area on GitHub on the way. The finished clip is stored for you on Cloudflare R2.

Your video and its frames are deleted as soon as the clip is made. If a job is interrupted, what it left behind is deleted within a day. We keep the time you ticked the box confirming you have the rights to the video and the consent of the people in it.

Songs

Songs you add to a composition stay in your browser. They are not uploaded to us.

What you share

Sharing is optional. While a clip or character is shared, anyone who opens the Sky Waker lobby can see your first name, its title, caption, picture and 3D preview, and how many hearts, saves and uses it has. Members can heart it, save it and use it in their own compositions. Counts are shown, never who gave them. You can unshare at any time; the Terms explain what happens to copies members already use.

Reports

If you report a post, we keep the reason, your note, the post, the time and who sent it, so one person's reports on a post count once. The author is never told who reported them. Sky Waker staff review reports. If you delete your account, the reports you made stay, without your name.

Anonymous reports

Sky Waker sends anonymous error reports and usage counts to its own servers: which kind of page and device, what went wrong, and how often Enter, Preload and Play were used. They carry no account, name, IP address or browser fingerprint, and are deleted after 30 days. You can turn them off in the lobby under System; that setting is kept in your browser.

In the headset

Sky Waker uses your hands to control it while you play. Hand positions are used live to run the effects, and are never recorded. A few readings, such as how far a pinch is closed, travel through our server to your own VJ screen and are not stored. Sky Waker doesn't ask your headset for its cameras, and doesn't scan your room.

Our Discord

If you join the Sky Waker Discord, our help bot, skyguide, reads the posts in the channels it can see and the messages you send it, so questions and problems reach our team and none wait unseen. It keeps a copy of each post (your Discord name and user number, the channel, the time and the words) for 90 days, then deletes it. Before it keeps a post, it blanks anything that looks like an access key or a private link. Its answers come from our own help notes, never from other people's posts. If our team answers your question, the question and the answer may be kept, without your name, to answer the same question next time. It also keeps your Discord user number and which welcome message it sent you, so it never sends one twice. Our team may use an AI assistant to help sort and answer what the bot collects. Discord keeps your posts under its own privacy policy.

Connection records

Our hosting companies (Fly.io and Cloudflare) see your IP address when you connect, and keep it in their logs for a short time to run and protect the service. Our own records (your folder, reports and anonymous reports) don't keep IP addresses.

In your browser

Some settings stay in your browser: Reduce flashing, the safety notice, Anonymous reports, the lobby feed and the tour. The VJ keeps autosaves there, and a headset keeps a copy of the clips it plays so Play runs smoothly. Your browser's site settings clear all of it. Cookies are in the Cookie Policy.

Who else handles it

  • Patreon: sign-in and membership. Patreon's own privacy policy covers your membership and payments.
  • Fly.io: runs the Sky Waker server and stores your folder, and runs the Discord help bot and its copy of posts, in the United States.
  • Cloudflare: stores clips (R2), our nightly backup copies and the status page.
  • RunPod: runs the GPUs that make clips from your frames, and keeps each job's details for a short time.
  • GitHub: the private transfer area for large frame sets, and these legal pages.
  • Instagram: only if you press Show the latest posts in the lobby.
  • Discord: only if you join our Discord, where our help bot reads the server's posts (see Our Discord above).
  • xAI: the AI assistant our team may use to sort and answer Discord posts.

We don't sell your information, show ads or use ad trackers. We share information when the law requires it, for example a report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.

How long we keep it

  • Your folder and clips: until you delete them or your account. If your membership ends, we keep everything, so you can come back or download it.
  • Videos and frames you upload: deleted when the clip is made; anything an interrupted job left behind, within a day.
  • Nightly backup copies: 30 days.
  • Anonymous reports: 30 days.
  • Discord posts our help bot copies: 90 days.
  • Deleting your account: everything you shared is hidden at once. Your account is deleted 30 days later, unless you sign in and press Keep my account first. Backup copies age out 30 days after that, so everything is gone within 60 days.

After a deletion we keep only:

  • a one-way code made from your account number, for about a month, so restoring a backup can't bring the account back;
  • the reports you made, without your name;
  • a post taken down for child safety, kept hidden for one year, as federal law requires (18 U.S.C. § 2258A(h)).

Your choices

  • Download my data (in the lobby): a zip of your account, your folder and what you shared. Download clips gives each clip.
  • Delete my account (in the lobby), as above.
  • Anonymous reports: turn them off in the lobby under System.
  • Reduce flashing: in the lobby under System, or in the VJ's timeline bar.

For anything else (to see, correct or delete what we hold, or to ask a question), email legal@dgmun.ai. We answer within 45 days. Depending on where you live, including California, other US states, the UK and the EU, you have further rights; our Privacy Policy lists them. Using them never changes how we treat you.

Teens and children

Sky Waker is for people 13 and over. If you are 13 to 17, you need a parent's or guardian's permission. Patreon sets its own age rules for paying for a membership.

If someone answers that they are under 13, we delete their account at once, and that browser can't answer again for 30 days. A parent or guardian who thinks a child under 13 has used Sky Waker can email legal@dgmun.ai, and we will delete the account.

Changes

When this notice changes, the date at the top changes. We announce big changes in the lobby or on Patreon.
