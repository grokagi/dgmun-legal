Sky Waker privacy notice

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Sky Waker XR™ is run by Avidigi Agency LLC. This notice says in plain words what the Sky Waker app keeps about you, who else handles it, how long it stays, and how to download or delete it. Our general Privacy Policy covers the rest. Where the two differ, this notice applies to Sky Waker.

What we keep, and why

Your account

When you sign in with Patreon, Patreon tells us your name, your email, your Patreon user number and your membership, so we know you are a member. We never see your Patreon password or your card.

Patreon also tells our server when your membership starts, changes tier, pauses or ends, with your Patreon user number, your tier and when it changed, so what you can use in Sky Waker follows your membership straight away. We keep that with your account; when a membership ends, the keys your own AI holds stop working.

Your age group

Sky Waker is for people 13 and over. Patreon checks age when someone joins, and Sky Waker does not ask for a birth date. If you are 13 to 17, you need a parent's or guardian's permission.

Your work

Your compositions, settings, characters, clips and the VFX modules you write are kept in your own private folder, for you. Other members can't see them unless you share them.

A VFX module you write or paste runs walled off inside your browser: it can't reach the internet, your files or your account, and it can only draw.

Your own splat scans

If you turn a splat scan of your own into a Tunnel, your browser reads the scan and boils it into a smaller set of points for the Tunnel. The scan itself never leaves your device. That set of points is kept in your browser and in your private folder on Cloudflare R2, so your headset can play it, until you press Remove or delete your account.

Clips made from your videos

When you make a clip, your video comes to our server, which turns it into still frames. The frames go to RunPod, whose GPUs run the depth model that turns them into a 3D point cloud. The model estimates how far away each part of the picture is. It doesn't recognise faces or identify anyone. Large sets of frames pass through a private storage area on GitHub on the way. The finished clip is stored for you on Cloudflare R2.

Your video and its frames are deleted as soon as the clip is made. If a job is interrupted, what it left behind is deleted within a day. We keep the time you ticked the box confirming you have the rights to the video and the consent of the people in it.

Clips made with Grok Imagine (from a picture or a video)

The picture-to-clip maker sends your picture, or stills from your video, to xAI. Its vision model suggests how the clip could move, and Grok Imagine makes the moving video from your picture and the words you chose. Our server turns that video into frames, which pass through a private storage area on Cloudflare R2 (cleared within a day) to RunPod's GPUs for the point cloud. Nothing in it recognises faces or identifies anyone.

Your picture or video and what the maker made from it stay in your private folder for 14 days, so you can rebuild the clip, then they are deleted (sooner once you have started six newer clips). The job's record (its name, the words you chose, its dates and a preview sheet of its frames) stays in your folder until you delete your account, and Download my data includes it. xAI handles what it receives under its own API terms.

Songs

A song you add to a composition is kept in your private folder on Cloudflare R2, so the composition plays its song on your other computers and tablets and in Sky Waker Lite on your phone. Only you can play it: sharing a clip never shares the song. It stays until you delete your account, and Download my data includes it.

Your own AI (agent keys)

If you press Make a key under Bring your AI in the lobby, or Your AI in the controller (the page you run a show from, also called the VJ controller), we make a key, or a private link with the key in it, for an AI assistant you use (such as Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or VS Code). We keep only a scrambled copy of each key, the name you gave it, and when it was made, last used and runs out; never the key itself, so we can't show it to you again.

An AI holding one of your keys reaches only your own things: it can read the names of your clips, songs, titles and compositions, write a new draft composition into your folder (never over one of yours), hand a VFX module to your bench (where it is checked like one you paste), and press pads and hits in your controller while you have it open. It can't press Play, make clips, share, download, delete or pay. What it sends passes through our server to your folder or your controller and is kept only as what it writes into your folder; a module it hands over waits for your bench and is deleted a day later. The AI you connect is your choice, and its company handles what it reads under its own terms. Treat a key like a password: anyone holding it can act on your files. Our server counts how often each of these abilities is used and whether it worked, was refused or failed: counts only, never the key, your account or what it was asked, deleted after 30 days.

Your own AI is a closed beta for people 18 or older. To apply, you tell us which AI you use and what you'd like it to make, confirm you are 18 or older and agree to the beta terms. We keep your answers, your name and email as Patreon gave them, when you applied and whether you were approved, in your own folder until you delete your account, and Download my data includes them. The Sky Waker team reads your application with your name and email, so we know who is asking.

TouchDesigner (a patch on your computer)

If you press TouchDesigner in the controller, we make an address for your show, for a TouchDesigner patch or our OSC bridge on your own computer. We keep one random number for it in your folder, with when it was made; the address seals that number and your account, so it never shows who you are. While something on that address is listening, the controller window you drive sends it numbers about your show through our server: its clock, the song's levels and beats, the name of each press you make, and the clips on stage (their names and times). Nothing is stored on the way, and nothing it sends back reaches your show or your folder. Anyone holding the address hears those numbers, so treat it like a password; New address stops every older one at once.

A clip's TD download is a zip of that clip's points and colours for TouchDesigner, saved to your own computer, like Download clips.

What you share

Sharing is optional, with one exception: on the Composer seat, each VFX module you save that passes its checks is shared to the lobby automatically. You agree to this the first time you save one; the time you agreed is kept in your folder and comes with Download my data. Delete a module to take it down. The Headliner seat keeps your modules private until you publish them.

While a clip, character or VFX module is shared, anyone who opens the Sky Waker lobby can see your first name, its title, caption, picture and 3D or moving preview, and how many hearts, saves and uses it has. Members can heart it, save it and use it in their own compositions. A shared VFX module's code is kept with the post and handed to a member only when they press Use. Counts are shown, never who gave them. You can unshare at any time; the Terms explain what happens to copies members already use.

Reports

If you report a post, we keep the reason, your note, the post, the time and who sent it, so one person's reports on a post count once. The author is never told who reported them. Sky Waker staff review reports. If you delete your account, the reports you made stay, without your name.

Closed beta feedback notes

If you are in the closed beta (the people we invite and the members approved for Your own AI), the controller's Feedback button lets you pin a note on any control. Before your first note, the controller shows what a note carries and asks you to agree. A note carries what you write, the control you pinned and its section, the show's state (its name, whether it is preloaded or playing, the selected block, the lit pads, and whether a headset or phone is linked), the Sky Waker build, your device and browser type, recent errors on that page, the last minute of what you pressed, and a picture of the Sky Waker window unless you remove it from the note. Text you typed into boxes, sign-in links, keys and codes are left out of the picture. A note never carries your camera, your microphone or anything outside that window.

Your notes are kept in your own folder until 90 days after the closed beta ends, then deleted. Download my data includes them, and Delete my account deletes them. The Sky Waker team reads them, and AI tools also read them to sort them and plan fixes: Grok, by xAI, run through Cursor. Nothing in a note is ever run as code, and no AI tool that can change Sky Waker reads your words. Planning summaries use a pseudonym instead of your name and may be kept in Notion. The closed beta is for people 18 or older.

The Feedback box

Every member has a Feedback button in the controller and at the foot of the lobby. It opens a plain box for telling us what is wrong, confusing or good. A note from the box carries what you write, the kind of note you picked, whether you ticked that it stops you from using Sky Waker, and whether you wrote it in the controller or the lobby. Unless your cookie choice is Necessary only, it also carries your device and browser type (one word each, such as "iphone" and "safari"), whether the screen is phone-sized, the light or dark theme and the Sky Waker build. It never carries a picture, a recording, anything from your screen, your shows or your files, or your camera or microphone. Before a note is kept, anything in it that looks like an email address, an access key or a sign-in link is blanked. You can send up to 10 a day.

Your notes and our replies are kept in your own folder until you delete your account. Download my data includes them, and Delete my account deletes them. The Sky Waker team reads them under a pseudonym instead of your name, and can answer you in the same box. The AI tools that read closed beta notes may also read these to sort them and plan fixes, under the same rules: nothing in a note is ever run as code, no AI tool that can change Sky Waker reads your words, and a planning summary uses the pseudonym and may be kept in Notion.

Anonymous reports

Sky Waker sends anonymous error reports and usage counts to its own servers: which kind of page and device, what went wrong, how often Enter, Preload and Play were used, how often each feature of the controller was used and whether by your hand or by something automatic such as the board, a timeline block or your AI (a count a day, never which show, clip, file or words), which kind of link brought a visit to the sign-in page (for example "an Instagram message to AI video creators") and what kind of membership that visit became, and, on a phone, which Sky Waker Lite features that kind of phone can run. They carry no account, name, IP address or browser fingerprint, and are deleted after 30 days. The first time you open the lobby, its cookie notice asks: Accept all sends them, Necessary only turns them off. Cookie choices at the foot of the lobby changes it; the choice is kept in your browser.

In the headset

Sky Waker uses your hands to control it while you play. Hand positions are used live to run the effects, and are never recorded. A few readings, such as how far a pinch is closed or which hand move you just made, travel through our server to your own controller screen and are not stored. Sky Waker doesn't scan your room, and asks your headset for its camera only if you turn on Camera for the monitor on the headset's start page; then your browser asks you first. With it on, the headset can also read your hands from that camera, on the headset itself, for Hand cues; only the move's name leaves it. If you turn on Occlude, the headset's depth sensing lets real things in your room hide the show; that depth picture stays on the headset.

The controller's Monitor and Sky Waker Lite's Live view show what your headset or your Desktop window draws. With Camera for the monitor on, the headset's picture shows its camera's view of your room behind the show; with it off, only the show. Those pictures pass through our server to your own screens while you watch, and are never stored.

Webcam Sorcery (a webcam or a phone camera)

If you open the Webcam Sorcery page and turn its camera on, your browser asks for the camera, and the page reads your hands from the picture on your own device. The picture never leaves that device and is never stored. Only a few numbers travel through our server to your own controller screen: how open each hand is, where it is in the frame, which hand move you made, and the outline of each hand as 21 points, so the show can draw it. They are not stored. The hand tracker runs in your browser from our own server; no outside service sees anything.

If you press Record on that page, the recording is made in your browser and saved to your own computer. With My mic on, your microphone opens only while a take records, and its sound goes only into that file. Sky Waker doesn't see or keep anything you record.

With Scene on, that page lays the Desktop window's picture over your camera. The picture passes straight between the two windows on your own computer, never through our server, and goes into a recording only if you record.

Sky Waker Lite (a phone)

Sky Waker Lite plays a composition and its song on your phone. If you put the show over the phone's camera, your browser asks for the camera, and the picture stays on the phone: it is shown behind the show and never sent or stored. AR uses the phone's own AR to find your floor; that stays on the phone too. With World anchor on, the phone follows the floor in its camera picture to keep the show standing in place, on the phone itself. Record saves the take to the phone, and Sky Waker doesn't see or keep it.

When your controller preloads a show onto your phone, the show comes to the phone through our server. With Camera hands on, the phone reads your hands from its own camera picture. Only numbers go through our server to your controller, never the picture: the hand move you made, a few readings such as how open each hand is, and, while Show hands is on, the outline of each hand as 21 points. They are not stored.

Our Discord

If you join the Sky Waker Discord, our help bot, skyguide, reads the posts in the channels it can see and the messages you send it, so questions and problems reach our team and none wait unseen. It keeps a copy of each post (your Discord name and user number, the channel, the time and the words) for 90 days, then deletes it. Before it keeps a post, it blanks anything that looks like an access key or a private link. Its answers come from our own help notes, never from other people's posts. If our team answers your question, the question and the answer may be kept, without your name, to answer the same question next time. It also keeps your Discord user number and which welcome message it sent you, so it never sends one twice. Our team may use an AI assistant to help sort and answer what the bot collects. Discord keeps your posts under its own privacy policy.

Connection records

Our hosting companies (Fly.io and Cloudflare) see your IP address when you connect, and keep it in their logs for a short time to run and protect the service. Our own records (your folder, reports and anonymous reports) don't keep IP addresses.

In your browser

Some settings stay in your browser: your cookie choice, Reduce flashing, the safety notice, the lobby feed and the tour. The controller keeps autosaves there, and a headset or phone keeps a copy of the clips it plays so Play runs smoothly (a headset asks its browser not to clear that copy on its own). Your browser's site settings clear all of it. Cookies are in the Cookie Policy.

Who else handles it

  • Patreon: sign-in and membership. Patreon's own privacy policy covers your membership and payments.
  • Stripe: takes the payment when you buy a clip pack, and sends your receipt. Your card details and email go to Stripe, never to us; we keep only the order number, the pack, its price and when you bought it.
  • Fly.io: runs the Sky Waker server and stores your folder, and runs the Discord help bot and its copy of posts, in the United States.
  • Cloudflare: stores clips, your songs and your Tunnels' points (R2), the short-lived transfer area for Grok Imagine clips' frames, our nightly backup copies and the status page.
  • RunPod: runs the GPUs that make clips from your frames, and keeps each job's details for a short time.
  • GitHub: the private transfer area for large frame sets, and these legal pages.
  • Instagram: only if you press Show the latest posts in the lobby.
  • Discord: only if you join our Discord, where our help bot reads the server's posts (see Our Discord above).
  • xAI: Grok Imagine and its vision model, when you make a clip from a picture or a video with them (see Clips made with Grok Imagine above), the AI assistant our team may use to sort and answer Discord posts, and Grok, which reads closed beta feedback notes and Feedback box notes to sort them and plan fixes (see Closed beta feedback notes and The Feedback box above).
  • Cursor: the tool our team runs Grok through when it reads closed beta feedback notes and Feedback box notes.
  • Notion: our team's planning notes. A summary of a closed beta feedback note or a Feedback box note may be kept there, with a pseudonym instead of your name.

We don't sell your information, show ads or use ad trackers. We share information when the law requires it, for example a report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.

How long we keep it

  • Your folder, clips and songs: until you delete them or your account. If your membership ends, we keep everything, so you can come back or download it.
  • Videos and frames you upload: deleted when the clip is made; anything an interrupted job left behind, within a day.
  • Pictures and videos you give the Grok Imagine maker: 14 days, or sooner once you have started six newer clips. The job's record stays with your account.
  • Nightly backup copies: 30 days.
  • Anonymous reports: 30 days.
  • Agent keys: until you turn one off; each runs out 30 days after your last visit, they all stop the next time you sign in after your membership ends, and they are deleted with your account.
  • Your TouchDesigner address: we keep its number until you press New address or delete your account, or the next time you sign in after your membership ends, and leave it out of the nightly backup copies. Each address you copy works for 400 days.
  • Discord posts our help bot copies: 90 days.
  • Closed beta feedback notes: in your folder until 90 days after the closed beta ends, then deleted.
  • Feedback box notes: in your folder, with our replies, until you delete your account.
  • Deleting your account: everything you shared is hidden at once. Your account is deleted 30 days later, unless you sign in and press Keep my account first. Backup copies age out 30 days after that, so everything is gone within 60 days.

After a deletion we keep only:

  • a one-way code made from your account number, for about a month, so restoring a backup can't bring the account back;
  • the reports you made, without your name;
  • a post taken down for child safety, kept hidden for one year, as federal law requires (18 U.S.C. § 2258A(h)).

Your choices

  • Download my data (in the lobby): a zip of your account, your folder and what you shared. Download clips gives each clip.
  • Delete my account (in the lobby), as above.
  • Anonymous reports: Necessary only in the lobby's cookie notice, or Cookie choices at the foot of the lobby.
  • Reduce flashing: in the lobby under System, or in the controller's timeline bar.
  • Your AI's keys: Turn off beside each one under Bring your AI in the lobby or Your AI in the controller. Cut your AI on the controller's timeline bar stops an AI pressing anything in your controller at once.

Teens and children

Sky Waker is for people 13 and over. If you are 13 to 17, you need a parent's or guardian's permission. Patreon sets its own age rules for paying for a membership.

If someone answers that they are under 13, we delete their account at once, and that browser can't answer again for 30 days. A parent or guardian who thinks a child under 13 has used Sky Waker can email legal@dgmun.ai, and we will delete the account.
