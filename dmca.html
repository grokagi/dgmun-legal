Copyright notices (DMCA), intimate images and child safety

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Sky Waker hosts the videos members upload, the clips made from them, and the posts members share in the lobby. This page says how to tell us that something there is yours, or shows you without your consent, and what we do about it.

Report a post

The quickest way is the post's Report button in the lobby. Choose It copies my work (copyright), It shows me, or someone I speak for, without permission, or the reason that fits. We may hide the post while we look.

Send a copyright notice

For a formal notice under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act, email legal@dgmun.ai with the subject line DMCA. It must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature (typing your full name is enough)
  2. The copyrighted work (or a representative list, if there are several)
  3. Where the material is on Sky Waker: the post's link, or the clip's name
  4. Your name, address, telephone number and email
  5. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent, or the law
  6. A statement that the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner

Our designated agent for notices of claimed infringement is Diana G. Munoz, Avidigi Agency LLC, 1 League #60511, Irvine, CA 92602, phone (858) 349-7850, email legal@dgmun.ai. The same listing is in the U.S. Copyright Office directory: Avidigi Agency LLC (active 13 October 2026).

What happens next

We hide the post from everyone and stop its shared copy playing. The member who shared it sees that it was taken down, and why. Their own file is not deleted.

Counter-notice

If your post was taken down and you believe that was a mistake or fair use, email legal@dgmun.ai with the subject line Counter-notice, including what 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3) requires:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature
  2. The material that was removed, and where it was before
  3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentification
  4. Your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, outside the United States, any judicial district where we may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the notice

We send it to the person who sent the notice. Unless they tell us first that they have filed a court action, we restore the post 10 to 14 business days after we receive your counter-notice.

Repeat infringers

An account that keeps sharing material it has no right to loses sharing, and may be closed.

Intimate images shared without consent

Report the post and choose A sexual or intimate image of someone shared without their consent, real or AI-made. It is hidden as soon as it is reported. If you are the person shown, or act for them, we remove it, and any copies we know of, within 48 hours of a valid request, as the TAKE IT DOWN Act requires. A valid request, by the Report button with your contact details in the note or by email to legal@dgmun.ai with the subject Intimate image, includes your signature (typing your full name is enough), where the image is, a short statement that it was shared without consent, and how to reach you.

Child safety

Report the post and choose It could put a child at risk. It is hidden as soon as it is reported. Child sexual abuse material is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children's CyberTipline and kept hidden for one year, as federal law requires.

False notices

Knowingly claiming that material infringes, or that it was removed by mistake, when it isn't, can make you liable for damages (17 U.S.C. § 512(f)).
